HR HANDBOOK - Effective 1 April 2026

CASUAL LEAVE
Every employee gets 12 casual leaves per year. Casual leave must be applied at least 2 days in advance through the HR portal. Unused casual leaves can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days. Casual leave cannot be combined with sick leave for more than 3 days.

SICK LEAVE
Every employee gets 10 sick leaves per year. The employee must inform the manager on the same day of absence. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

EARNED LEAVE
Employees earn 15 days of earned leave per year, credited monthly. Earned leave can be encashed at year end up to 10 days. Earned leave of more than 5 consecutive days needs approval from the department head.

MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
Maternity leave is 26 weeks with full pay. Paternity leave is 10 working days and must be taken within 3 months of the birth. Adoption leave follows the same rules as maternity leave.

PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
The company observes 12 public holidays and 2 optional holidays per year. The holiday list is published on the HR portal every January.

WORK FROM HOME
Work from home is allowed on Fridays with manager approval. More than 2 work from home days in a week needs department head approval.
