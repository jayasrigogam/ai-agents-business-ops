WORKLOAD & PRODUCTIVITY REPORT
========================================
Hours +10.1% but tasks completed -1.5% and output per hour -10.2% over 12 months.
Productivity peaks near 45 h/week; beyond ~45 h output per hour drops sharply.
Biggest bottleneck teams: Support, Operations.
Top productivity drivers: AbsentDays, OvertimeHrs, WeeklyHours.

RECOMMENDED INTERVENTIONS:
1. Cap planned hours at 45/week; rebalance work in Support and Operations (hire or redistribute).
2. Treat rising absenteeism as an early-warning signal (top productivity driver): HR check-ins and wellbeing support.
3. Run engagement check-ins for the 'Overloaded / burning out' segment before absenteeism rises.
4. Track output per hour (not hours worked) as the headline productivity KPI.
