LEAVE POLICY
Every employee gets 12 casual leaves and 10 sick leaves per year. Casual leave must be applied at least 2 days in advance through the HR portal. Unused casual leaves can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.

ATTENDANCE POLICY
Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Arriving after 10:00 AM three times in a month counts as one half-day leave. Work from home is allowed on Fridays with manager approval.

REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Travel and meal expenses must be submitted within 15 days of the expense with original bills. Claims above 5000 rupees need approval from the department head. Reimbursements are paid in the next monthly salary cycle.

DRESS CODE
Business casual from Monday to Thursday. Casual wear is allowed on Fridays.
